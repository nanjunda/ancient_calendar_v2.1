🔭 Planetary Harmony & Synodic Resonances

The Mayan calendar is not merely a tracking system for days, but a sophisticated instrument for measuring planetary synodic periods. One of their most astounding discoveries was the resonance between Venus and the Sun: 5 Venus synodic cycles (584 days each) align exactly with 8 solar years (365 days each), meeting at the precise interval of 2,920 days. This mathematical lock allowed the Maya to predict heliacal rises with near-perfect accuracy over centuries, as documented in the Dresden Codex.
